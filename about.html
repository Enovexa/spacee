Our Story

A decade of dance, now under new light

Space Dance & Art Academy is a Brisbane-based dance and fitness academy with a growing presence in South Brisbane and North Brisbane. The academy has been shaping dancers for over a decade, and since 2025 has flourished under new ownership led by Francis Joseph.

It was built on a simple vision — a welcoming, affordable and supportive space where children, young people and adults could discover the joy of dance, build confidence, stay active and experience the thrill of performing on stage.

What began as a passion for bringing people together through movement has grown into a vibrant community of more than 150 dance students, alongside an expanding adult dance and Zumba fitness community — training across Bollywood, Bharatanatyam, Semi-Classical, Indian Folk, Hip-Hop, Fusion and Zumba Fitness.

At Space, we believe dance is about far more than choreography. It builds confidence, improves fitness, develops discipline and teamwork, connects us with culture, creates friendships, and — most importantly — brings people together.

Our students take what they learn beyond the classroom, performing at multicultural festivals, community celebrations, dance competitions, cultural programs and academy productions — including our own Space Annual Concert. Whether you're taking your first steps or refining years of experience, there's a place for you here.

Meet Our Founder

Francis Joseph

Founder & Director

Francis Joseph is the Founder and Director of Space Dance & Art Academy and has a genuine passion for dance, Indian arts, multiculturalism and community development.

He brings experience in coordinating multicultural programs, event management, dance and cultural activities, community events and team coordination.

One of his key strengths is bringing people together — instructors, dancers, parents, volunteers, community organisations and businesses — to create successful events and meaningful performance opportunities.

Francis is passionate about providing opportunities for both children and adults to develop confidence and experience the excitement of performing on stage.

Outside the academy, Francis is a Nurse Practitioner, bringing extensive professional experience in healthcare, leadership, communication, teamwork and community service.

His vision for Space is not simply to build a dance school, but to develop a supportive dance and fitness community where everyone feels welcome and has an opportunity to participate, perform and belong.

Meet Our Team

Bonny Kurian

Team Leader  ·  Dance Trainer  ·  Choreographer  ·  Zumba Instructor

Bonny Kurian is a passionate dancer, choreographer and fitness instructor with experience across a wide range of Indian dance and fitness styles.

He is known for his friendly, flexible and approachable teaching style, working with students of different ages, abilities and fitness levels — understanding that everyone learns at a different pace.

His teaching focuses on gradually developing confidence, coordination, rhythm, fitness, expression and performance skills, all while ensuring students genuinely enjoy the learning experience.

Bonny plays a key role in choreography, instructor coordination, student development, Zumba programs and the artistic direction of Space performances.
