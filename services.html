Our Courses & Programs

Classes For Every Age, Every Style

From your child's first dance steps to adult fitness and competition-ready choreography, explore the full range of classes and programs on offer across both our Brisbane branches.

Explore Our Programs

Dance Styles & Programs

Bollywood Dance

Energetic and expressive Bollywood choreography focusing on rhythm, coordination, expression, fitness and stage presence.

Age Groups: Children, teenagers and adults
Skill Levels: Beginner to experienced
Performance Opportunities: Yes

Bharatanatyam / Classical Dance

Structured Indian classical dance training focusing on foundational technique, posture, rhythm, expression and progressive development.

Age Groups: Children and teenagers
Skill Levels: Beginner onwards

Semi-Classical Dance

A combination of classical Indian foundations with contemporary and Bollywood influences.

Age Groups: Children, teenagers and adults
Skill Levels: Beginner onwards

Indian Folk Dance

Energetic and colourful dances inspired by India's diverse regional traditions.

Age Groups: Children and teenagers
Performance Opportunities: Yes

Hip-Hop & Fusion

Modern choreography combining contemporary movement, Bollywood, Hip-Hop and other styles.

Age Groups: Children and teenagers
Skill Levels: Various levels

Ladies Bollywood Dance

A friendly and supportive program for women who want to learn Bollywood and Semi-Classical dance while improving fitness, coordination and confidence.

Previous Dance Experience: Not required
Performance Opportunities: Available for interested participants

Zumba Fitness

Fun, energetic group fitness sessions combining music, movement and dance-inspired exercise.

Suitable For: Adults
Fitness Level: Beginner to experienced
Dance Experience: Not required

Our Locations

South Brisbane

Programs are offered in the Hillcrest, Acacia Ridge area and surrounding locations.

Classes include: Children's Bollywood, Semi-Classical, Fusion and Classical dance programs.

North Brisbane

Programs are offered in the Aspley / Bridgeman Downs area and surrounding locations.

Classes include: Zumba, adult dance and developing children's/teenage dance programs.

Class Information

Class Schedules

Class schedules vary according to location, age group and program type.

Visit our Timetable page for the most up-to-date schedules that are updated regularly without disrupting the main website content.

Children's programs generally operate in line with Queensland school terms, while adult programs may operate on an ongoing basis.

Batch Size & Course Duration

Batch sizes are kept manageable to allow instructors to provide appropriate guidance and support to all participants.

Exact maximum numbers vary depending on the class, age group, and venue to ensure quality instruction and personalized attention.

This approach enables us to maintain the high standard of teaching and create a supportive learning environment for all our dancers.
